Acme Corp — Remote Work Policy (v3)

Company details:
- Total employees: 248
- HR Head: Priya Nair
- CEO: Rohan Mehta
- Registered address: 14 MG Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560001

1. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
2. Core hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST; employees must be reachable during this window.
3. Remote work requests must be approved by a direct manager at least 2 business days in advance.
4. Employees working remotely for more than 2 consecutive weeks must notify HR.
5. Company laptops must use the VPN when accessing internal tools from outside the office.
6. This policy does not apply to interns, who are required to work on-site 5 days per week.
